# NexusShop — Terms of Service & Privacy Policy (Pilot)

Last updated: 2026-08-31 · Operator: Lucas Guzmán (independent founder, Argentina)

## 1. What NexusShop is
NexusShop is a zero-commission marketplace that lets small
merchants publish a public store and sell through WhatsApp.
During the pilot, NexusShop does NOT process payments:
buyers and sellers arrange payment and delivery directly.

## 2. Who is who
- Operator: Lucas Guzmán, independent founder (Argentina).
- Merchant: independent seller; solely responsible for
  products, prices, fulfillment and legal compliance.
- Buyer: contracts directly with the merchant.

## 3. Merchant rules
Lawful products only. Accurate descriptions and prices.
Prohibited: weapons, counterfeit goods, regulated drugs,
adult content, anything illegal in Pakistan or Argentina.

## 4. Fees
0% commission during the pilot. Any future fees will be
announced 30 days in advance.

## 5. Data we collect
Merchant: store name, WhatsApp number, emoji, product list
(name, price). Buyer: none stored by the platform.

## 6. How we use data
To display the public store and enable WhatsApp contact.
We never sell data. No third-party trackers.

## 7. Your rights
Message the operator on WhatsApp to access, correct or
delete your store and data at any time.

## 8. Data storage
Data is stored on the operator's systems (NexusBase).
Pilot data residency: Render cloud (US). A Pakistan
data-residency plan is in progress.

## 9. Liability
NexusShop is a showcase intermediary. Transactions happen
between buyer and merchant. Liability is limited to the
maximum extent permitted by law.
